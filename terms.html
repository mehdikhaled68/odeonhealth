Digital Credentials

Pending Decision: The ownership model for digital credentials is under review. Two positions are presented below for editorial resolution.

Option A — ODEON Health-issued, clinician-licensed (recommended):

ODEON Health issues and owns each digital credential as a certification mark. Upon verified completion of the relevant assessment level, ODEON Health grants the clinician a personal, non-transferable, irrevocable licence to use, display, and share their credential for professional identification purposes. This licence is not affected by the clinician's departure from the deploying institution.

Option B — Clinician-owned:

Upon verified completion of the relevant assessment level, the digital credential is issued to and owned by the clinician outright. ODEON Health retains the right to revoke a credential in the event of verified fraud, material misrepresentation, or a change to the underlying assessment standard.

Regardless of ownership model, the following conditions apply:

  • Credentials are issued in accordance with the Open Badges / IMS Global standard and are verifiable by third parties via the ODEON Health credential registry.
  • A credential may be revoked by ODEON Health in the event of verified fraud or material misrepresentation in the completion of an assessment.
  • Credentials do not constitute a medical licence, regulatory approval, or endorsement by any professional body unless explicitly stated.
  • ODEON Health reserves the right to update credential standards; clinicians holding existing credentials will be notified of any material changes.
